# WriteHub AI - Feature & Capability Overview

## 🚀 **Executive Summary**
WriteHub AI is a next-generation customer engagement platform that blends advanced AI automation with the personal touch of human support. It empowers businesses to create intelligent, brand-aware agents that can handle customer inquiries, process complex tasks, and seamlessly hand off to human team members when needed—all through a customizable, beautiful chat interface.

---

## 🌟 **Core Platform Features**

### **1. Unified Dashboard & Navigation**
Access your command center through an intuitive, modern interface designed for productivity.
*   **Dashboard Overview:** Get a pulse on your operations with real-time metrics on credit usage, active agents, and recent activity.
*   **Smart Navigation:** Quickly jump between building agents, managing conversations, and configuring your workspace.

### **2. The Agent Builder Studio**
Create powerful AI agents without writing a single line of code.
*   **Persona Design:** Customize your agent's name, role, and personality instructions to match your brand voice perfectly.
*   **Model Selection:** Choose from top-tier AI models (Gemini Pro/Flash) optimized for speed or reasoning capabilities.
*   **Visual Widget Customization:**
    *   **Brand Styling:** Visual editor to set primary colors, welcome messages, and descriptions.
    *   **Interactive Preview:** See exactly how your widget will look on your site before deploying.
    *   **Feature Toggles:** Enable/disable features like voice input, file uploads, and lead collection with one click.

### **3. The Support Inbox (Human Escalation)**
A dedicated workspace for your human support team to collaborate with AI.
*   **Live Conversation Monitoring:** Watch chats happen in real-time.
*   **Seamless Handoff:** When an AI agent gets stuck or a user requests human help, the chat is instantly routed to the Inbox.
*   **Human Reply:** Jump into any conversation to provide manual support. The AI automatically pauses to let you take over.
*   **Contextual History:** See the full chat history, including files and images shared, so you never have to ask "How can I help?" twice.

### **4. Specialized Agent Tools & Integrations**
Empower your agents to *do* work, not just talk about it.
*   **Visual Vision:** Agents can analyze images uploaded by users (e.g., "What's wrong with this product in the photo?").
*   **Document Intelligence:** Agents can read and synthesize information from uploaded text files, PDFs, and data files (CSV/JSON).
*   **App Integrations (via Composio):** Connect to 90+ external apps like:
    *   **Productivity:** Google Calendar (book meetings), Slack (send alerts).
    *   **Development:** GitHub (check issues, analyze code).
    *   **CRM:** Salesforce, HubSpot (lookup customers, create leads).

### **5. Administration & Settings**
Complete control over your organization and security.
*   **User Profile:** Manage your personal account details and preferences.
*   **Organization Management:**
    *   **Team Collaboration:** Invite agents and team members to your workspace.
    *   **Roles & Permissions:** Secure your workspace with Owner/Member roles.
*   **API Management:** Generate and revoke API keys securely to integrate the chat widget on any website.
*   **Billing & Credits:**
    *   **Transparent Usage:** Track every credit spent on AI interactions.
    *   **Easy Top-Ups:** Purchase credits instantly via Stripe to keep your agents running smooth.
